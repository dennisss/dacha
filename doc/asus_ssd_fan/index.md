

- Adapter to add extra fans to an [ASUS Hyper M.2 V2 Card](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07NQBQB6Z). This reduces SSD temperature overall and improves the directionality of the air flow (air only flows from inside the case to outside the back).
- Print the adapter with supports (I used PETG).
- Unscrew the stock fan and tape over the fan hole in the heatsink.
- Use with 40x40x10mm 12V blower fans like [this](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07MS1DBZ7).
- The fans should screw onto the 3d printed adapter with the screws that come with the fans (no nuts required).
- The adapter is a friction fit onto the heatsink of the ASUS card. Be sure to verify that the adapter is centered along the edge of the heatsink so that all the air blows into the hole.
- Reduced my NVMe temperatures from ~68 C to ~60 C with around 5W of load per NVMe.
